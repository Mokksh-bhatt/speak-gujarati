Duolingo does not teach Gujarati. Most language apps skip it, and general courses rarely teach the everyday words families use at home. We are making short lessons built around real family moments: the phone call, the dinner table, the wedding, the garba night. It is not ready yet. Below are three sample lessons, so you can judge it before you give us anything.

Try three lessons now

1. Calling your Ba

જય શ્રી કૃષ્ણ બા, કેમ છો?
Jay Shri Krishna Ba, kem chho?
Hello Ba, how are you?
તમારી તબિયત કેવી છે?
Tamari tabiyat kevi chhe?
How is your health?
મને તમારી બહુ યાદ આવે છે.
Mane tamari bahu yaad aave chhe.
I miss you a lot.

2. At the dinner table

જમવાનું બહુ સરસ છે.
Jamvanu bahu saras chhe.
The food is really good.
થોડું વધારે આપો.
Thodu vadhare aapo.
Give me a little more.
બસ, પેટ ભરાઈ ગયું.
Bas, pet bharai gayu.
Enough, I'm full.

3. Garba night

ચાલો ગરબા રમવા!
Chalo garba ramva!
Come, let's play garba!
તમારી ચણિયાચોળી બહુ સરસ છે.
Tamari chaniya choli bahu saras chhe.
Your chaniya choli is beautiful.
મને શીખવાડશો?
Mane shikhvadsho?
Will you teach me?

What only this course will do

Planned

Call rehearsal

Type what you want to tell her this week. Get five sentences, practise them aloud, then press "call now". Progress is counted in real calls, not streaks.

Planned

Ba becomes the teacher

She sends one voice note on WhatsApp, as she already does. It arrives as "today's word from Ba", with the meaning and a way to answer her in Gujarati.

Planned

Lessons in your Ba's own voice

Forward a few of her voice notes. We turn them into lessons: her words, her accent, her favourite phrases. And her voice stays with the family.

Planned

Subtitles for your grandmother

Put a real call on speaker. See what she just said, and two ways to reply.
